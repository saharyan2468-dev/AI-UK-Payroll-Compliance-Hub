Terms and conditions

UK Payroll compliance copilot provides general payroll estimates for information and planning. Results are not legal, tax, financial or professional payroll advice and may not reflect every individual circumstance or the latest legislation.

You are responsible for checking inputs, reviewing assumptions and confirming results with current HMRC, GOV.UK and The Pensions Regulator guidance. Do not enter personal or special-category employee data. By using this site, you agree to use it only for lawful purposes and accept that estimates may contain errors.

Data policy

This tool is designed not to ask for NI numbers, bank account details, addresses, full dates of birth, names, or sensitive employee information. Only the figures needed for an estimate should be entered.

Calculations run in your browser memory. This static demo does not implement local storage, cookies, a server-side endpoint, encryption, or a retention/deletion service. Values and results can remain visible in the page while it is open; closing or refreshing the page normally discards the page instance, but this demo does not claim guaranteed deletion or server-side controls. Do not enter personal or sensitive employee data.

For the most current legal position, consult official UK government guidance or a qualified adviser.
