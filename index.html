Terms and conditions

UK Payroll compliance copilot provides general payroll estimates for information and planning. Results are not legal, tax, financial or professional payroll advice and may not reflect every circumstance or the latest legislation.

Check inputs and confirm results with current HMRC, GOV.UK and The Pensions Regulator guidance. Do not enter personal or special-category employee data.

Data policy

This tool does not ask for NI numbers, bank account details, addresses, full dates of birth, names, or sensitive employee information. Only figures needed for an estimate should be entered.

Inputs and results are used to provide the calculation and explanation. Data entered is automatically deleted from the site once it has been used for the calculation, and session data is cleared when you close or refresh the page. We do not sell inputs for advertising.
